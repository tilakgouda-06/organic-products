# Organic Store - Copywriting Guide

## Brand Voice Guidelines

**Core Attributes:** Friendly, trustworthy, earthy, transparent, encouraging  
**Avoid:** Overly salesy language, corporate jargon, pushy CTAs  
**Focus:** Connection, nature benefits, sustainability, community

---

## Option 1: Warm & Approachable

### 1. Hero Section - Contact Page

**Headline:** Let's Grow Together 🌱

**Sub-headline:** Have a question about our harvest, a request for a bulk order, or just want to share your love for pure ingredients? We're here to listen — like old friends catching up over chai.

### 3. Support Narrative (Trust Signal)

> **We're Just a Message Away**
> 
> Every question matters to us — whether you're curious about our sourcing, need help with an order, or simply want to share your organic journey. We read every message ourselves and typically respond within 24 hours (often sooner!). Because at Organic Store, you're not just a customer — you're family.

---

### 4. Footer Brand Boilerplate (About Snippet)

> **From Karnataka's Forests to Your Home**
> 
> We handpick 100% certified organic treasures from the lush forests of Karnataka, working directly with local farmers who share our passion for chemical-free living. Every product tells a story of sustainable harvest, ancient wisdom, and unwavering commitment to purity. Join our growing family of conscious consumers choosing nature's best — because you deserve nothing less than what the earth provides at its most generous.

## Implementation Notes

### For Contact Page Integration:

1. **Hero Section:** Replace current "Get in Touch" headline with chosen option
2. **Form Labels:** Update all form field labels and placeholders
3. **Trust Signal:** Add the support narrative paragraph below the form or in a sidebar
4. **Footer:** Replace existing footer brand text with chosen boilerplate

### Additional Microcopy Suggestions:

**Success Message after form submission:**
- Warm: "Thank you! Your message has been received with gratitude. We'll be in touch soon! 💚"
- Minimalist: "Message sent successfully. We'll respond within 24 hours."

**Error Message:**
- Warm: "Oops! Something went wrong. Please check your details and try again."
- Minimalist: "Submission failed. Please verify your information and retry."

**Loading State:**
- Warm: "Sending your message with care..."
- Minimalist: "Submitting..."
